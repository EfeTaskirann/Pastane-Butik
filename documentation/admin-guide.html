Dashboard

The dashboard at /admin/dashboard.php is your daily command centre. At a glance you get six stat cards (today's orders, revenue, average ticket, new messages, pending refunds, low stock), a revenue sparkline for the last fourteen days, a list of the five most recent orders, and inbox-unread messages. The cards refresh automatically every thirty seconds.

The dashboard respects role-based access. Viewers see the same layout but without action buttons. Editors can click through to orders and messages but cannot access user management. Admins see everything.

Products

Navigate to Menu → Products or /admin/urunler.php. This is where your menu lives. You can create, edit, archive, feature, and hide any product.

FieldDescription
Name (isim)Customer-facing product name. Up to 80 characters.
CategoryForeign key to the categories table. Required.
DescriptionLong-form description shown on the product detail card. Plain text, auto-paragraph on display.
PricePrimary price in Turkish Lira. Two decimal places.
PortionsOptional per-size pricing (e.g. Small, Medium, Large). Defined on the Categories page.
ImageJPG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB. The system automatically generates thumbnail variants.
FeaturedShows the product in the homepage carousel.
ActiveToggle off to hide from the menu without deleting.

Click a product row to open the edit form. Image uploads run through a dedicated sanitiser that strips EXIF data and verifies the MIME against the actual file contents to prevent polyglot uploads.

Categories

Categories are the top-level groups on the public menu. Create as few or as many as you want; the default seed ships with five. Each category has a display order (sira) that determines left-to-right placement on the menu, and an optional icon.

Hierarchy is single-level by design - subcategories are explicitly not supported to keep the customer flow fast. If you need to group further, use product tags.

Orders

The orders screen at /admin/siparisler.php is the single source of truth for every delivery, pickup and in-house order. Filters let you narrow by date range, status, payment method, and channel (menu or QR table).

Each row expands to reveal the customer's details, line items, unit prices, totals, delivery address (for online orders) and a payment event trail. Action buttons on the right allow you to:

  • Mark as preparing, ready, delivered or cancelled.
  • Print an 80 mm thermal receipt directly from the browser.
  • Reassign the order to a different waiter or kitchen.
  • Trigger a refund (gateway-specific; for cash orders the operation is logged only).

Status transitions write to the activity log and, for online orders, trigger the matching customer notification (email and optional SMS).

Messages

Contact form submissions accumulate at /admin/mesajlar.php. Each message can be replied to inline (the reply is emailed back via your configured SMTP) or archived. Unread messages are counted in the dashboard header badge.

Calendar

The calendar view at /admin/takvim.php is an operational timeline of every order grouped by delivery or pickup slot. Each cell shows order count and total revenue, and clicking a cell opens the detail drawer. This is particularly useful during busy periods such as Mother's Day, Valentine's Day or the first week of Ramadan.

You can drag-drop an order from one slot to another to reschedule, and the system will record the change in the activity log plus notify the customer.

Tables

Physical tables are managed at /admin/masalar.php. Each table has a name (typically a number), a capacity hint, and a unique QR token. Print the QR poster directly from the interface - the generated PDF includes the table name, the QR code itself, and a short "scan to order" hint in your chosen language.

QR tokens. Tokens are random 32-byte values, rendered as hex. They are safe to expose publicly; they only identify the table, not the account. If you suspect a token was photographed and abused, you can rotate it from the same screen.

Kitchen queue

The kitchen view at /admin/mutfak.php is a large-screen-friendly live queue of orders that are in the "preparing" state. It polls the server every thirty seconds and highlights orders that have been pending for longer than your configured SLA.

Waiter view

The waiter screen at /admin/garson.php is the companion view to the kitchen. It groups orders by table, shows the total due per table, and lets the waiter mark an item as delivered to the table. The most common workflow is: guest scans QR, places order, kitchen prepares, waiter carries items, waiter taps "delivered". Optimised thanks to the Sprint 4 N+1 fix (single batch query for all tables).

Reports

Reports live at /admin/raporlar.php. You can export any of them to CSV or PDF.

  • Daily revenue - line chart for the last thirty days.
  • Monthly revenue - bar chart for the last twelve months.
  • Top products - sorted by quantity sold or by revenue.
  • Customer segments - grouped by frequency: new, returning, VIP.
  • Hour-of-day heatmap - identify your peak hours.

Activity log

Every sensitive action writes a row to the security_events table and is browsable at /admin/activity-log.php. Filters include user, event type, IP address and date range. Pagination is 25 rows per page; the full JSON payload is available behind a "Details" modal.

Typical events captured include: administrator login (success and failure), password change, role change, 2FA enable or disable, product create or delete, order status override, settings change, and backup download.

Settings

Settings are grouped into five tabs at /admin/ayarlar/.

General

Site title, contact phone, address, working hours, tax registration number, delivery radius, minimum order amount. Saved to the ayarlar table which is cached for five minutes.

Security / 2FA

Covered in depth in Configuration. Each administrator must enable 2FA individually. Backup codes are single-use and are shown only once.

Mail

Confirm SMTP settings, test send, set default From address, toggle email templates on and off (order confirmation, shipping notification, password reset).

SMS

Choose driver, enter API credentials, test send, toggle SMS templates on and off. Failed sends are logged and retried up to three times.

Backup

The backup tab shows the most recent database backups, their size, and their age. You can trigger a manual backup with one click. Automatic backups are scheduled through bin/cron/db-backup.php; a sister job bin/cron/backup-monitor.php warns you if the latest backup is older than 25 hours and raises a critical alert at 48 hours.

# Suggested crontab entries:
10 3 * * *   cd /var/www/pastane && php bin/cron/db-backup.php
*/30 * * * * cd /var/www/pastane && php bin/cron/backup-monitor.php
0  4 * * *   cd /var/www/pastane && php bin/cron/log-rotate.php

Users (RBAC)

User management lives at /admin/ayarlar/kullanicilar.php (admin role only). Three built-in roles are available:

RoleTypical userWhat they can do
adminOwner, IT leadFull access to every screen, all settings, user management.
editorStore managerManage products, categories, orders, messages, reports; cannot change user accounts or system settings.
viewerAccountant, auditorRead-only access to orders, reports and activity log.
Change the default admin password immediately after installation. The credentials generated by db-seed.php are logged to disk and should be considered temporary.
